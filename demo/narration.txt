Clone-in-the-Loop is a Q M fork that predicts what you would ask your agent next.

G Brain retrieves relevant sessions from your Codex and Claude history, plus explicitly shared team history. That context personalizes the suggested prompt. Press Tab once to accept it, then Enter to send it.

Across Research, Product, and Marketing, this becomes a repeatable workflow.

For ongoing work, press Tab twice to enable Clone mode. Your Clone gives the next instruction. Q M executes it. Your Clone reviews the result and asks for what is missing.

Here, it catches a weak call to action and asks for a concrete example. It requests a specific correction, then checks the revised result. This loop continues until you press Stop.

Goals show where the work stands. The Inbox suggests what to tackle next. Switch to Clone Garry for another perspective on your team's pitch.

Personal and team memory stay separate, and you can inspect the sources behind each suggestion. Your agent executes. Your Clone carries your judgment.
